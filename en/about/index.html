How we started

A calling, a flame

NewFire was born in 2020 from the vision of a group of believers in Mexico City who dreamed of a ministry that would reach every corner of the country. In a world where information is abundant but trustworthy spiritual resources are scarce, we felt God calling us to do something different.

Today we share Bible studies, video series, prayers, and biblical novels in Spanish and English, so the Word can reach more people.

Our mission

To equip the people of God across the United States, Mexico, and South America with resources that transform lives.

Every resource we create, every guide we publish, and every community we form has one single purpose: that more people across the United States, Mexico, and South America come to know, love, and serve Jesus Christ.
